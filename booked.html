Before Your Visit

A few simple steps to get ready

01

Arrive 15 minutes early

Please plan to arrive about 15 minutes before your appointment time. It gives you time to check in, finish any paperwork and settle in, so your full appointment is spent on you.

02

Come makeup-free

For any treatment on the face, please arrive with clean skin: no makeup, heavy moisturizer or sunscreen on the treatment area. If you are coming straight from work, just allow a few extra minutes to cleanse before we begin.

03

Complete your forms

Look for an email from our secure patient portal (OptiMantra) with your intake and consent forms. Completing them before you arrive keeps check-in quick. You can also sign in to the portal anytime to finish them.

04

Bring your ID and payment

Please bring a photo ID and your preferred form of payment. Interested in spreading out the cost? Ask us about our payment plan options.

05

Know your health history

Be ready to share your current medications and supplements, allergies, recent cosmetic treatments, and whether you are pregnant or breastfeeding. It helps your provider plan safe, personalized care.

06

Plan your day

Some treatments can cause temporary redness, swelling or bruising. If you have a wedding, photos or a big event coming up, let us know so we can help you time your treatment.

Treatment Tips

Helpful prep by treatment

Botox & fillers

  • Arrive makeup-free.
  • Avoiding alcohol for about 24 hours beforehand may help reduce bruising.
  • Tell us about any blood thinners or supplements. Never stop a prescribed medication without talking to your doctor.

Laser, BBL & hair reduction

  • Avoid tanning, sunburn and self-tanner for about two weeks beforehand.
  • For laser hair reduction, shave the area 24 hours before. Please do not wax or pluck.
  • Arrive with clean skin and no lotion on the treatment area.

Facials, peels & microneedling

  • Arrive makeup-free.
  • Pause retinoids and exfoliating acids for a few days beforehand, unless your provider advises otherwise.
  • Let us know about any recent sunburn, breakouts or skin sensitivity.

Waxing

  • Let hair grow to about 1/4 inch (roughly two to three weeks of growth).
  • Skip exfoliating or tanning the area the day of your appointment.

Brows & lashes

  • Come with clean brows and lashes: no mascara or brow makeup.
  • Remove contact lenses before a lash service if they bother your eyes.

Good to know

These are general tips. Your provider will confirm anything specific to your treatment and health history. If you are unsure about anything, call or text us before your visit.
