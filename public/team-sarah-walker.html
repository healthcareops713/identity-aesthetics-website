Meet Sarah Walker, BS, MSN, APRN, FNP-C

Clinical experience meets practical nutrition.

Advanced Provider and Nutritionist

Sarah Walker, BS, MSN, APRN, FNP-C, is a dedicated nurse practitioner with more than 20 years of healthcare experience. Her clinical focus includes hormone optimization, weight management, health promotion and evidence-aware peptide therapy when clinically appropriate.

Before entering nursing, Sarah earned a Bachelor of Science in Nutrition with an emphasis in sports nutrition and disease prevention and management. She later earned her Master of Science in Nursing. This combination of nutrition education and advanced clinical practice supports a comprehensive approach to patient health and wellness.

As a former collegiate athlete, Sarah understands the roles that nutrition, physical activity, recovery and overall health can play in performance. She brings that perspective to patient care, using evidence-based strategies to help people pursue sustainable health, body-composition and performance goals.

Sarah approaches emerging therapies thoughtfully—as one possible part of a broader plan rather than a shortcut. Her care emphasizes appropriate screening, realistic expectations, nutrition, activity, recovery and ongoing clinical follow-up.

She is committed to helping patients feel and function at their best while taking an active role in their long-term health. Her blend of advanced clinical experience, nutrition education and personal athletic background offers a distinctive perspective on weight management, hormone health, recovery and physical performance.
