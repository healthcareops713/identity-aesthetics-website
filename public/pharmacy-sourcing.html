Due-diligence standard

How we evaluate a pharmacy relationship

Confirm legal identity and facility

Match the pharmacy name, dispensing location, contact information and applicable permit records. Similar branding does not make separate facilities legally identical.

Verify permission to dispense

Review current licensure relevant to the dispensing facility and the patient's state. Authority can differ by location and prescription.

Require a lawful prescription process

Prescription medication follows an appropriate clinical evaluation and valid authorization. A website order or payment alone is not a prescription.

Review regulatory and quality records

Consider FDA registration where applicable, inspection observations, warning letters, recalls and open agency actions without treating an allegation or observation as a conviction or proven patient injury.

Explain compounded-drug limits

Compounded drugs are not FDA-approved. FDA does not review them for safety, effectiveness or quality before marketing in the same way it reviews approved drug products.

Common questions

Medication sourcing FAQ

Are compounded medications FDA-approved?
No. FDA does not approve compounded drugs. Compounding may be appropriate for an identified patient under applicable law and clinical circumstances, but it does not make the preparation FDA-approved or equivalent to an approved product.
Does 503B registration mean FDA approval?
No. It means a facility has registered with FDA as an outsourcing facility and is subject to applicable federal requirements and inspection. Registration is not product approval, endorsement or a guarantee of compliance.
Can I receive medication without a prescription?
No prescription medication is provided solely because a patient requests or pays for it. Appropriate evaluation, lawful authorization and pharmacy requirements apply.
Why might the dispensing pharmacy change?
The appropriate pharmacy may depend on the medication, formulation, patient location, current license authority, availability and the prescriber's clinical judgment. Ask which pharmacy will fill your specific prescription before dispensing.
Is AnazaoHealth independently confirmed as LegitScript certified?
This page does not claim a verified pharmacy certification based only on a supplied name or badge. Use the linked live LegitScript checker to confirm current status for anazaohealth.com. Certification status can change.
