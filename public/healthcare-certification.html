Clinical safeguards

How prescription treatments are handled

Identity Aesthetics does not present prescription treatment as an ordinary retail purchase. Clinical eligibility and applicable law determine whether treatment is appropriate.

Evaluation before treatment

Prescription treatments require evaluation by a qualified, licensed medical professional. A website visit, form submission, quoted starting price or appointment request is not a prescription and does not guarantee treatment.

No prescription medicine without authorization

Identity Aesthetics does not offer prescription medication without a valid prescription or other lawful clinical authorization. Product selection, dose and treatment plan are determined only after appropriate review.

In-office procedure boundaries

Injectable procedures such as botulinum toxin and dermal filler treatments are performed in person. Telehealth may support evaluation or follow-up where lawful, but cannot replace the in-person examination and administration required for an injectable procedure.

Licensed geography

Telehealth is offered only when the patient is physically located in a state where the treating professional is authorized to provide care. Current site availability is described for Texas, North Carolina and South Carolina and remains subject to provider licensure, law and clinical determination.

Medication sourcing

When medication is prescribed or a compounded preparation is clinically appropriate, fulfillment must follow a valid prescription and applicable pharmacy law. Compounded drugs are not FDA-approved products, and their use must not be represented as equivalent to FDA approval. Review our pharmacy sourcing and medication safety record.

Truthful, non-guaranteed claims

Pricing, candidacy, timing and results vary. Advertising and educational content must not guarantee outcomes, conceal material limitations or describe an investigational or compounded product as FDA approved.

Important domain notice

Certification follows the approved domain—not the appearance of a badge.

The LegitScript verification linked above is for 713botoxme.com. A badge shown on a different preview or development address does not extend that certification to the other domain.

Paid healthcare campaigns should use the certified production domain as the final landing-page domain, and the advertiser must separately complete any Google Ads healthcare certification required for the campaign, location and service. LegitScript approval supports verification but does not guarantee ad approval.

Advertising transparency

What Google evaluates

Healthcare ad review is broader than website markup. Google may evaluate the advertiser account, the certified domain, ad and keyword language, geographic targeting, landing-page content and third-party information. Botox and botulinum toxin are monitored restricted prescription-drug terms.

Campaign safeguards

  • Complete Google healthcare certification before running covered campaigns.
  • Use the same certified domain in the application, ads and final landing URLs.
  • Target only locations where the service can lawfully be provided.
  • Do not use restricted prescription-drug terms without the required permission.
  • Do not use advertiser-curated health audiences or health-based remarketing.
  • Keep pricing accurate, current and qualified by consultation requirements.
