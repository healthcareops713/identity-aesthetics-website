The short answer

What is Tirzepatide for Medical Weight Management?

Tirzepatide activates GIP and GLP-1 receptors. FDA-approved Zepbound is indicated for chronic weight management in eligible adults, with additional labeled use for moderate-to-severe obstructive sleep apnea in adults with obesity, alongside nutrition and physical activity.

Concerns it may address

  • Obesity or overweight with qualifying weight-related conditions
  • Appetite regulation and sustained weight-management support
  • Obesity-related obstructive sleep apnea when the labeled criteria are met
  • Patients prepared for long-term monitoring and behavior change
Candidacy

Who may—or may not—be a candidate

Who may be considered

Eligibility depends on current labeling, BMI, related conditions, medications and risk profile. The prescriber confirms the indication, coordinates diabetes medicines and assesses gastrointestinal, gallbladder and thyroid history.

When treatment may not be appropriate

Tirzepatide is contraindicated with a personal or family history of medullary thyroid carcinoma or MEN 2 and with serious hypersensitivity. Pregnancy and important gastrointestinal, pancreatic, gallbladder, kidney, eye and medication issues require review.

Informed choice

Important risks, limitations and alternatives

Risks & limitations

Common effects include nausea, diarrhea, vomiting, constipation, abdominal discomfort and injection-site reactions. Important risks include pancreatitis, gallbladder disease, dehydration-related kidney injury, severe gastrointestinal effects, allergy and low blood sugar with certain diabetes drugs. Labeling includes a boxed thyroid C-cell tumor warning.

Alternatives to discuss

  • Intensive lifestyle and nutrition treatment
  • Semaglutide or another FDA-approved anti-obesity medication
  • Sleep-apnea treatment when indicated
  • Bariatric-surgery evaluation for eligible patients
Clinical judgment

Why provider experience matters

Safe care requires more than escalating a dose. Experienced supervision verifies sourcing, coordinates diabetes or sleep-apnea treatment, protects nutrition and lean mass, and recognizes when side effects or inadequate benefit require a change.

Common questions

Tirzepatide for Medical Weight Management FAQ

Is compounded tirzepatide FDA approved?
No. Compounded drugs are not FDA-approved. FDA has raised concerns about unapproved GLP-1 products, dosing errors and quality.
Is Mounjaro the same as Zepbound?
Both contain tirzepatide, but they have different FDA-approved indications and labeling. The prescribed product must match the diagnosis and treatment plan.
Can I combine tirzepatide with another GLP-1 drug?
Do not combine or switch medicines without prescriber direction. Zepbound labeling limits coadministration with other tirzepatide or GLP-1 products.
