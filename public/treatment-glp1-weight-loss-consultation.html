The short answer

What is GLP-1 Weight-Loss Consultation?

A GLP-1 consultation determines whether prescription weight-management treatment is appropriate and which option, if any, best fits your health, goals, risks, access and preferences. It includes diagnosis, medication review, source verification, nutrition and a monitoring plan.

Concerns it may address

  • Repeated weight regain or difficulty achieving clinically meaningful loss
  • Obesity-related blood pressure, lipid, glucose, sleep or joint concerns
  • Questions about semaglutide, tirzepatide, orforglipron or other FDA-approved medications
  • Side effects, plateaus or transition from an unapproved product
Candidacy

Who may—or may not—be a candidate

Who may be considered

Adults who meet an FDA-approved medication’s eligibility criteria may be considered. The visit also helps people who are not candidates understand safer alternatives.

When treatment may not be appropriate

A consultation may identify pregnancy, contraindicated thyroid history, severe gastrointestinal disease, pancreatitis or gallbladder risk, eating disorder, medication interaction or another issue that changes or postpones treatment.

What happens during treatment

Evaluate

We review weight trajectory, prior treatments, nutrition, activity, sleep, medications, pregnancy plans and medical conditions.

Plan & treat

The clinician determines whether testing, records, primary-care coordination or another diagnosis is needed.

Review & follow up

Options, product sourcing, expected benefits, side effects, cost and monitoring are compared. Prescription is issued only when clinically appropriate.

Informed choice

Important risks, limitations and alternatives

Risks & limitations

The main risk of skipping a complete assessment is receiving the wrong drug, dose or source. GLP-1 medicines have gastrointestinal and serious medical risks, and counterfeit or unapproved versions can add dosing and quality hazards.

Alternatives to discuss

  • Structured nutrition and behavioral treatment
  • Non-GLP-1 FDA-approved anti-obesity medication
  • Management of sleep, endocrine or medication-related contributors
  • Bariatric-medicine or surgical referral
Clinical judgment

Why provider experience matters

Obesity is a chronic medical condition, not a willpower problem. Experienced physician-supervised care addresses contraindications, comorbidities, lean-mass preservation, realistic rate of change and long-term maintenance.

Identity Aesthetics approach

  • Diagnosis and anatomy before a product or device
  • Conservative planning with candid limitations
  • Licensed-provider supervision and complication readiness
  • In-office and telehealth boundaries stated clearly

Common questions

GLP-1 Weight-Loss Consultation FAQ

Will I receive a prescription at the first visit?
Only if the clinician has enough information and treatment is appropriate. Records, labs or additional evaluation may be needed first.
Do you guarantee a specific medication?
No. The purpose of the visit is to determine the safest option, which may be a different medication or no medication.
Can the entire program be telehealth?
Many visits may be virtual in eligible states, but local labs, vital signs, examination or referral may be needed.
