Common questions

Katy location FAQ

Where is Identity Aesthetics in Katy?

Our Katy location is at 1227 Grand W Blvd Ste B207-B, 2nd Floor, Katy, TX 77449. Call 713-268-6963 for directions or parking guidance before your first visit.

Do you offer Botox in Katy?

Yes. Botox® Cosmetic and other FDA-approved neurotoxins including Dysport®, Xeomin® and Jeuveau® are offered at our Katy location. Units are not interchangeable between products, so your provider will discuss which is appropriate for you.

How do I book an appointment in Katy?

You can request a consultation online or call 713-268-6963. New patients start with a consultation so a provider can review your health history and goals before recommending treatment.

Can I be seen by telehealth instead?

Some services, including weight-management and hormone consultations, may be handled by telehealth for patients in Texas, North Carolina and South Carolina. Injectable treatments require an in-person visit to Katy or another location.
