Access With Dignity

Accessibility Statement

Identity Aesthetics wants patients and visitors with disabilities to access our digital information and services with dignity and independence.

Reviewed July 22, 2026

Our commitment

We work to improve website usability for people using keyboards, screen readers, zoom, voice input and other assistive technologies. Our goals include clear headings, meaningful links, sufficient color contrast, text alternatives for informative images and responsive layouts.

Need assistance?

If a page, document, booking tool or embedded feature is difficult to use, call or text 713-268-6963 or email info@713botoxme.com. Please identify the page or task and the format or assistance that would be useful. We will make a reasonable effort to provide the information or service through an accessible alternative.

Third-party features

Some booking, financing, mapping, translation, social-media and virtual-preview experiences are operated by third parties. We do not control every aspect of those interfaces, but feedback about access barriers is welcome so we can help identify an alternative and communicate concerns to the provider.

Ongoing improvement

Accessibility is an ongoing practice. Content and technology change, and occasional barriers may occur. We review feedback and prioritize issues that prevent access to essential information, communication and appointment pathways.

When contacting us

Include the web address, what you were trying to do, the barrier encountered and your preferred contact method. Please do not email highly sensitive medical details through ordinary email.
