The sensation facial

What is a Korean Glass Skin Facial?

“Glass skin” describes a complexion that appears smooth, clear-looking, intensely hydrated and luminous. It is a beauty ideal—not a single standardized medical procedure. At Identity Aesthetics, the experience is personalized after a skin assessment and may blend gentle cleansing, carefully selected surface exfoliation, concentrated hydration, a soothing mask, facial massage and barrier-supportive finishing products.

The result is an elegant, event-ready polish: skin that feels softer and looks refreshed without pretending that one facial can permanently erase pores, scars, pigment disorders or the natural texture of real skin.

Ideal goals to discuss

  • Dull, tired-looking skin
  • Dehydrated or tight-feeling skin
  • Dry surface buildup or rough texture
  • A smoother-looking makeup canvas
  • A luminous finish before a special event
  • A gentler alternative to aggressive resurfacing
The ritual

Five layers of thoughtful luminosity

Every step is adjusted to your skin; not every client receives every technique or active ingredient.

Assess & cleanse

We review sensitivity, breakouts, medications, active skincare and recent procedures before a gentle, thorough cleanse.

Refine

An appropriate enzyme or other mild exfoliation may lift dull surface buildup without automatically stacking aggressive treatments.

Replenish

Hydrating and humectant-rich layers are selected to support a plump, supple appearance and comfortable skin feel.

Soothe

A calming mask and restorative finishing care help reduce the look of temporary dryness and support the skin barrier.

Illuminate & protect

The ritual closes with moisturizer and broad-spectrum sunscreen as appropriate, leaving a refined—not greasy—glow.

Customization is the luxury: optional enhancements such as dermaplaning, a peel or device-based treatment are considered only after assessing your skin and should be deliberately sequenced rather than automatically combined.
Candidacy

Who may—or may not—be a candidate

Who may be considered

Adults with intact skin who want hydration, mild surface refinement and a temporary luminous finish may be candidates. The treatment can often be softened for dry or sensitive skin, but the current condition of the barrier determines what is appropriate.

When treatment may be modified or postponed

Active infection, open wounds, sunburn, uncontrolled eczema or rosacea, inflamed acne, active dermatitis, a recent aggressive peel or laser, or a known allergy to planned ingredients may require modification, postponement or medical evaluation.

Benefits, limitations and possible reactions

What clients love

  • A fresh, dewy and well-rested appearance

What to understand

Results vary and are cosmetic and temporary. Possible reactions include redness, stinging, dryness, irritation, allergy, breakout or pigment change—especially when exfoliation is too aggressive or incompatible products are combined. This facial does not cure acne, melasma, rosacea or eczema and does not replace medical diagnosis or deeper remodeling treatments.

Why expertise matters

The glow should never cost you your barrier

The artistry is knowing when to add—and when to stop. An experienced esthetician considers skin type, pigment response, active products, allergies, recent treatments and the condition of the barrier before choosing exfoliation or layering products.

Common questions

Korean Glass Skin Facial FAQ

Is this one standardized Korean procedure?
No. “Korean facial” and “glass skin” describe a beauty philosophy and desired cosmetic finish, not one universally standardized procedure. Identity Aesthetics customizes the steps to your skin.
Will my skin actually look like glass?
The phrase describes a smooth, hydrated and luminous appearance. Real skin retains pores and natural texture, and no ethical facial should promise permanent or digitally perfect skin.
Is it suitable before an event?
Often, but first-time clients should allow enough lead time to observe how their skin responds. Tell us your event date so we can select a conservative plan.
Can I combine it with dermaplaning or a peel?
Sometimes, but only when your skin barrier and recent treatment history make the combination appropriate. More exfoliation is not automatically better.
How should I care for my skin afterward?
Use gentle cleansing, moisturizer and broad-spectrum sunscreen. Follow your esthetician's instructions about retinoids, acids, heat, exercise and other potentially irritating products or activities.
Is it available through telehealth?
No. The assessment and facial are performed in office.
