Glo2Facial Questions

What to know before you glow

Personalization begins with a professional skin assessment and a conversation about your goals, sensitivities, medications and recent treatments.

Can Glo2Facial treatments be customized?

Yes. Glo2Facial can be personalized through interchangeable OxyPods and adjustable treatment settings selected for your skin concerns and goals.

What makes the treatment regenerative?

The treatment is designed to support the skin’s natural renewal processes through exfoliation, oxygenation and targeted ingredient infusion. Your provider can explain what this means for your individual skin plan.

Can Glo2Facial be used on sensitive skin?

Glo2Facial settings can be adjusted, but suitability depends on an individual consultation, current skin condition, sensitivities and recent procedures.

How often can I receive a Glo2Facial?

Many providers recommend treatment every four to six weeks, depending on your skin, goals and individualized plan.

How is an OxyPod selected?

Your provider selects the customization after reviewing your skin concerns, desired result and any relevant sensitivities or treatment history.

How is Glo2Facial different from a standard facial?

Glo2Facial combines three technologies in one treatment: Oxfoliation™, LUX ultrasound and hands-free massage, along with a targeted OxyPod customization.
