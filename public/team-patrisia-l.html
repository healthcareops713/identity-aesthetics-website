Meet Patrisia L., CLT

Confidence, warmth and unmistakable personality.

Aesthetic Consultant | Facial Rejuvenation

Patrisia brings confidence, warmth and unmistakable personality to the Identity Aesthetics experience. As an Aesthetic Consultant and Certified Laser Technician, she helps patients navigate facial-rejuvenation options while supporting the high standards, organization and attention to detail that keep the practice running smoothly.

Known for her energetic leadership style, candid communication and commitment to getting things done, Patrisia approaches every part of the patient experience with pride—whether she is helping someone explore aesthetic goals, coordinating facility operations or supporting the team behind the scenes.

She combines “CEO energy” with a willingness to handle whatever the day requires. Fueled by iced lattes, high standards and her signature question—“Did you do that yet?”—Patrisia helps make Identity Aesthetics polished, welcoming and exceptionally organized.
