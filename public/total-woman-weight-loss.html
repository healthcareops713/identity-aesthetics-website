The Identity Method

One journey. Six connected conversations.

Your plan is built around what your body needs—not a social-media protocol or a medication-only subscription.

01

Clinical baseline

We review your history, prior attempts, medications, pregnancy plans and risk factors. Your clinician orders baseline chemistry and other testing based on your health—not a generic internet checklist.

02

Medication, when appropriate

GLP-1–based medication may be considered after candidacy and contraindications are reviewed. It is one tool inside a broader plan, with ongoing monitoring and adjustment.

03

Nutrition you can live with

Sarah Walker, APRN, FNP-C, Advanced Provider and Nutritionist, brings a bachelor’s degree in nutrition to practical guidance on protein, hydration, fiber, meal size and timing, symptoms and muscle preservation.

04

Peptide evaluation—not a “stack”

Additional peptide-based therapy is considered only when separately indicated, lawful, appropriately sourced and supported by credible evidence. We explain FDA approval and compounded-drug limitations clearly.

05

Face, skin and body planning

Substantial weight loss can reveal laxity or facial volume changes. Ultrasound-based skin tightening, laser rejuvenation, neuromodulators or fillers may be discussed after an anatomy-based assessment—never automatically.

06

Women’s wellness

Confidence can open conversations about dryness, discomfort or urinary leakage. Those symptoms deserve diagnosis; we can evaluate appropriate options and referrals rather than making one-treatment promises.

Nutrition Is Clinical Care

Food does not “cancel” GLP-1 therapy—but it can change how you feel.

There is no universal forbidden-food list. For some patients, large or high-fat meals, concentrated sweets, highly processed foods or alcohol can worsen gastrointestinal symptoms or work against their goals. Sarah helps turn the physiology into an eating pattern that fits your life, supports lean tissue and adapts as your appetite changes.

Safety Before Speed

Baseline chemistry is our standard of care.

Identity Aesthetics requires an appropriate baseline evaluation before accepting a patient into medical weight-management care. Testing is individualized and may include metabolic, kidney, liver, glucose-related, thyroid or other measures based on your history and clinician judgment.

A precise note about thyroid safety

Not every GLP-1 medication requires universal thyroid blood testing. Current labels focus on personal or family history of medullary thyroid carcinoma and MEN 2; routine calcitonin testing or thyroid ultrasound has uncertain value for early detection. We screen the person—not just a lab panel—and investigate symptoms or history appropriately.
