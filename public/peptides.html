Start With How You Feel

Are you feeling unlike yourself?

Many people seek a wellness consultation because gradual changes have begun affecting everyday life. You do not need to diagnose yourself before asking for help.

01

Persistent fatigue or a noticeable decline in everyday stamina.

03

04

Body changes

06

Difficulty concentrating or not feeling as mentally sharp as usual.

Symptoms are a starting point—not a diagnosis.

These concerns can have many possible causes and do not necessarily mean peptide therapy is appropriate. Your consultation is designed to understand the whole picture and determine whether peptide therapy, another treatment or additional evaluation makes the most sense.

What to Expect

Peptide therapy FAQs

Does having these symptoms mean I need peptide therapy?

No. Fatigue, sleep disruption, body-composition changes and reduced vitality can have many causes. A consultation helps identify possible contributors and determine whether peptide therapy, another evidence-based option or further evaluation may be appropriate.

Can my consultation be completed by telehealth?

Eligible patients who are physically located in Texas, North Carolina or South Carolina at the time of their visit may begin with a secure telehealth consultation from home. Availability, prescribing and follow-up requirements depend on your medical needs and applicable state rules.

Are all peptide treatments FDA-approved?

No. FDA approval applies to specific products, formulations, routes and medical uses. Some products discussed online are compounded, investigational or sold only for research. Your clinician should explain the regulatory status and quality of any medication being considered.

How will I know whether treatment is helping?

Before treatment, you and your clinician should identify realistic goals and a follow-up plan. Response, adverse effects and relevant clinical information can then be reviewed to decide whether treatment should continue, change or stop.
