Meet Dallas Alvey, MD, DDS

Where medical knowledge meets aesthetic precision.

Medical Leadership · Aesthetic, Hormone and Wellness Medicine

With more than nine years of experience in aesthetic, regenerative and wellness medicine, Dallas Alvey, MD, DDS, brings an unusually comprehensive clinical perspective to Identity Aesthetics. His practice focuses on aesthetic medicine, hormone optimization, peptide therapy and individualized strategies designed to help patients look, feel and perform at their best.

Dr. Alvey’s education as both a medical physician and a dentist provides him with an advanced understanding of facial anatomy, structural relationships, function and proportion. This dual medical and dental background is especially valuable in aesthetic medicine, where small differences in anatomy, balance and treatment placement can meaningfully influence a patient’s outcome.

His surgical training further informs his methodical approach to patient evaluation, procedural planning and clinical safety. Rather than recommending the same treatment for every patient, Dr. Alvey considers the individual’s anatomy, medical history, concerns and long-term goals before developing a personalized plan.

As part of the medical leadership at Identity Aesthetics, Dr. Alvey helps oversee clinical protocols, physician-supervised treatment plans and quality standards across the practice. His approach emphasizes informed decision-making, appropriate patient selection and natural-looking results that preserve each person’s unique features.

Beyond aesthetic medicine, Dr. Alvey works with men and women seeking physician-guided support for concerns involving energy, body composition, hormonal health, physical performance and overall wellness. When appropriate, treatment may include hormone optimization, peptide therapy, lifestyle guidance and ongoing clinical monitoring.
