The short answer

What is Jeuveau®?

Jeuveau® is a prescription botulinum toxin type A treatment used to temporarily reduce muscle activity that contributes to expression lines. Identity Aesthetics selects dose and placement according to your anatomy, movement and desired degree of softness—not a one-size-fits-all pattern. It is FDA-approved for temporary improvement of moderate-to-severe glabellar lines in adults.

Concerns it may address

  • Frown lines between the brows
  • Forehead lines and crow’s-feet when clinically appropriate
  • A tired, tense or stern expression caused by dynamic muscle movement
  • Preventive softening for selected adults who still want natural expression
Candidacy

Who may—or may not—be a candidate

Who may be considered

Adults seeking temporary improvement in expression lines may be considered after an in-person facial assessment. A good candidate understands that results are temporary, asymmetry is possible, and the safest dose depends on muscle strength, anatomy, prior response and treatment goals.

When treatment may not be appropriate

Treatment may be deferred for pregnancy or breastfeeding, active infection at an injection site, known allergy to an ingredient, certain neuromuscular disorders, or symptoms that require medical evaluation. Tell the provider about all medications, supplements, prior toxin products and any swallowing or breathing problems.

Informed choice

Important risks, limitations and alternatives

Risks & limitations

Expected effects can include temporary redness, tenderness, bruising, swelling or asymmetry. Rare but serious botulinum-toxin risks include swallowing, speaking or breathing difficulty and spread of toxin effect. Product units are not interchangeable. Treatment cannot be guaranteed and must follow an individualized examination.

Alternatives to discuss

  • No treatment or skincare for surface-quality goals
  • Another FDA-approved botulinum toxin, recognizing units cannot be converted directly
  • Dermal filler or biostimulator when volume—not muscle movement—is the primary concern
  • Laser or resurfacing treatments for pigment, texture and fine etched lines
Clinical judgment

Why provider experience matters

Small differences in depth, angle, dose and muscle selection change the outcome. An experienced medical injector can distinguish dynamic lines from volume loss, protect brow and eyelid function, plan around asymmetry and recognize complications early.

Identity Aesthetics approach

  • Diagnosis and anatomy before a product or device
  • Conservative planning with candid limitations
  • Licensed-provider supervision and complication readiness
  • In-office and telehealth boundaries stated clearly

Common questions

Jeuveau® FAQ

Will I look frozen?
The goal can be subtle softening with natural movement. Your desired finish, anatomy and dose are discussed before treatment; no injector can promise an exact appearance.
Can I switch toxin brands?
Sometimes, but each product has its own formulation and labeled dosing. Units are not interchangeable, so the provider plans a new dose rather than using a simple conversion.
When should I call the office?
Contact the practice promptly for concerning weakness, eyelid or brow changes, severe headache, vision changes or any unexpected symptom. Seek emergency care for trouble breathing, swallowing or speaking.
