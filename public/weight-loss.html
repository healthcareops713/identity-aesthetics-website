The Medications

Current options, one future to watch

S

Semaglutide

The GLP-1 receptor agonist behind Ozempic® and Wegovy®. Current Wegovy labeling includes injection and adult tablet options with product-specific instructions and indications. A clinician determines which formulation, if any, fits the patient.

Semaglutide Guide
FDA-Approved Oral GLP-1
O

Foundayo (orforglipron)

A once-daily oral GLP-1 receptor agonist FDA-approved in 2026 for long-term weight reduction in eligible adults. It can be taken with or without food, but requires product-specific screening, titration, interaction review and ongoing medical monitoring.

Review the 2026 GLP-1 Tracker
Dual-Action
T

Tirzepatide

The dual GIP/GLP-1 agonist behind Mounjaro® and Zepbound®. By activating two metabolic pathways, tirzepatide has shown some of the strongest weight loss results ever studied. Weekly injection, titrated by your provider for comfort and results.

Tirzepatide Guide
Triple-Action · Phase 3 News
R

Retatrutide — “Triple-G”

An investigational once-weekly medicine that targets GIP, GLP-1 and glucagon pathways. Lilly has reported positive topline results from multiple Phase 3 trials. Retatrutide is not FDA-approved, cannot legally be sold for human use and is not available for routine prescribing.

Questions

GLP-1 FAQs

Am I a candidate for GLP-1 weight loss?

GLP-1 therapy is generally appropriate for adults with a BMI of 30+, or 27+ with a weight-related condition — but candidacy is always determined by your provider after reviewing your health history and labs. Certain conditions, including a personal or family history of medullary thyroid cancer or MEN2, rule out treatment.

What results can I expect?

In clinical trials, patients on semaglutide lost on average around 15% of body weight, and tirzepatide patients lost up to roughly 20% over 68–72 weeks alongside diet and lifestyle changes. Individual results vary — consistency and follow-up matter.

What are the common side effects?

Nausea, constipation and fatigue are most common, especially during dose increases. We titrate slowly and stay in close contact to keep side effects manageable. Your provider will review the full safety profile at your consultation.

How much does the program cost?

Program pricing depends on medication and dose. We'll walk you through exact pricing at your consultation, and financing is available through Cherry. No insurance is required.

Can I do everything by telehealth?

Yes — consultations, follow-ups and medication shipment can all be handled remotely for patients in Texas, North Carolina and South Carolina.
