The Right Care Setting

Convenient care, clear boundaries

Telehealth makes eligible medical consultations easier to access, but it does not replace every type of care. Your provider will determine whether virtual care is appropriate and whether laboratory work or an in-person examination is needed.

Injectables, laser procedures, facials and body-contouring treatments still require an appointment at an Identity Aesthetics location.
