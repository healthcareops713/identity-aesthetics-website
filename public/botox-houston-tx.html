Express service

Express toxin visits in Houston

For patients whose plan is already established, this location runs a streamlined toxin appointment—straightforward, without the additional consultation time a first visit requires.

Who it suits

Returning patients on a known product and dose who want maintenance treatment without a full planning appointment.

What stays the same

Treatment is still performed by a qualified injector, and your provider still reviews changes to your health history, medications and pregnancy status before treating.

When it is not appropriate

New patients, anyone changing product or dose, and anyone with a new concern start with a standard consultation instead. Ask when you book and we will schedule the right visit type.

Common questions

Houston location FAQ

Where is Identity Aesthetics in Houston?

Our Houston location is at 6806 Long Point Rd, Suite C, Houston, TX 77055. Call 713-268-6963 for directions or parking guidance before your first visit.

Do you offer Botox in Houston?

Yes. Botox® Cosmetic and other FDA-approved neurotoxins including Dysport®, Xeomin® and Jeuveau® are offered at our Houston location. Units are not interchangeable between products, so your provider will discuss which is appropriate for you.

How do I book an appointment in Houston?

You can request a consultation online or call 713-268-6963. New patients start with a consultation so a provider can review your health history and goals before recommending treatment.

What are your hours in Houston?

We are open Tuesday through Friday from 9am to 6pm and Saturday from 10am to 2pm. We are closed Sunday and Monday. Call 713-268-6963 to confirm availability before travelling.

Can I be seen by telehealth instead?

Some services, including weight-management and hormone consultations, may be handled by telehealth for patients in Texas, North Carolina and South Carolina. Injectable treatments require an in-person visit to Houston or another location.
