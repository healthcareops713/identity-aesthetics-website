# Identity Aesthetics Design System

This file records the visual contract established by the original Claude design. Future work should extend this system without changing its character.

## Brand character

- Luxury medical aesthetics and precision wellness
- Refined, warm, editorial, medically credible
- Inclusive of women and men
- Restrained rather than flashy; natural rather than overdone

## Typography

- Display and headings: `Cormorant Garamond`, weights 400–600
- Body, navigation, labels, and buttons: `Jost`, weights 300–500
- Headlines use generous scale, light-to-medium weight, and occasional italic gold-gradient emphasis
- Navigation and eyebrow labels use uppercase text with wide tracking

## Color tokens

- Canvas: `#faf8f3`
- Secondary surface: `#f3efe6`
- Cards: `#ffffff`
- Primary text: `#2c2620`
- Muted text: `#756b5c`
- Dim text: `#a19684`
- Gold: `#a5823d`
- Gold highlight: `#8f6d2c`
- Gold deep: `#7c5e26`
- Metallic gradient: `linear-gradient(120deg, #d9bc7f 0%, #b3924f 45%, #8c6d33 100%)`
- Espresso: `#1b1712`
- Footer espresso: `#14110d`

## Layout and components

- Maximum content width: `1180px`
- Standard horizontal padding: `24px`
- Card radius: `14px`; feature panels: `18px`
- Standard section spacing: `96px`; compact sections: `64px`
- Cards use fine neutral borders and subtle warm shadows
- Buttons are pill-shaped, uppercase, widely tracked, and either metallic gold or outlined
- Photography should sit in existing editorial fields with a soft ivory fade behind copy

## Photography direction

- High-definition, photorealistic luxury editorial style
- Warm ivory, champagne, taupe, charcoal, and restrained gold
- Natural skin texture and realistic bodies; no excessive retouching
- Subjects generally occupy the right half with clear copy space on the left
- Mature, diverse women and men, usually ages 35–55
- No text, logos, watermarks, visible needles, blood, or sensational clinical imagery

## Preservation rule

Do not change the established fonts, color values, navigation treatment, page width, card language, button styling, or overall spacing unless the user explicitly requests a redesign. New pages and sections should reuse the same classes, tokens, and visual rhythm.
