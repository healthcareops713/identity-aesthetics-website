Laser Expertise

Six laser technologies, one trained eye

Different lasers work at different wavelengths and depths. Part of Patrisia’s role is helping match the right technology to your skin type and goals.

Diode lasers

Widely used for laser hair reduction. The light targets pigment in the hair follicle to reduce regrowth over a series of sessions.

Laser hair removal

YAG lasers

A longer wavelength that reaches deeper into the skin. It is often chosen for hair reduction on deeper skin tones and for certain vascular and pigment concerns.

Laser hair removal

Thulium lasers

A gentle fractional resurfacing option often used to brighten tone, soften sun damage and refine texture with relatively short downtime.

Laser rejuvenation

CO2 lasers

Ablative resurfacing for deeper wrinkles, acne scars and uneven texture. It can deliver dramatic results and needs more recovery time.

Laser rejuvenation

Erbium lasers

Precise resurfacing that removes thin layers of skin with less surrounding heat, for fine lines, texture, sun damage and scars, often with a shorter recovery than CO2.

Sciton resurfacing

Fractional lasers

Treat a fraction of the skin at a time, leaving healthy tissue between columns to speed healing, for texture, fine lines and scarring.

Sciton BBL and ProFractional

Which laser is used depends on your skin type, goals and the devices available at each location. Your consultation confirms the right option and whether a laser is safe for you.
